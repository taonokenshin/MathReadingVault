# Exact mathematical continuation

## Reader preface

State the book/topic, learning purpose, prerequisites, source location and scope. Distinguish the source's statement, an existing argument, a cited input and what this session actually attempted. Historical records inform the route; the learner's present aims control it.

## Current mathematical question

Write the precise claim with all hypotheses and actual inputs. Identify the decisive lemma or calculation and why it matters for the learning goal.

## Work and qualification

Record the argument in natural mathematical order. Separate what was reused, newly supplied, checked in a finite example, conditional on a cited theorem, or left as a gap. Preserve an unsuccessful route when it explains the obstruction. Do not infer a general statement from a numerical guard.

## Stop and resume

- Last completed step and its stable note/heading.
- Exact remaining implication, branch or calculation.
- Required source/input and the first operation to perform next time.
- Candidate adjacent question if this step finishes early.
- Any dependency or source boundary that must stay fixed.

Report direct mathematical work, source comparison, maintenance and interruptions separately. A session status is operational evidence, not proof certification. A private log may retain its controller receipt; public study histories should omit operational identities.
