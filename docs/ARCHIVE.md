# Marcus and Neukirch public reading archive

The [download directory](../downloads/README.md), documented by the [dated release](https://github.com/taonokenshin/MathReadingVault/releases/tag/study-edition-2026-10-20), provides a public export of the English edition frozen on 20 October 2026. It contains 977 Neukirch articles and 175 Marcus articles, plus 584 and 540 normalized educational session records respectively. All 1,152 articles have reader prefaces; eight early session records also preserve full mathematical texts that lacked resolved standalone articles. Chapter/section guides, a result/exercise finder, cross-book paths, source hashes and study-assessment guidance support lookup.

These numbers count files, including successive versions, historical errors and corrections. They are not counts of distinct proofs or accepted controller occurrences. The private source edition additionally preserved 2,299 original files; those raw originals are not redistributed publicly. The public export excludes account details, exact workflow identities, original comparison ledgers, runtime, automation memory, PDFs, page images and caches.

Neukirch follows Chapters I–VII, including VII §13 Density Theorems. The 14 October completion record describes first reading and systematic verification as complete with explicit boundaries, records source coverage of 293 printed exercises, and states that not all results were independently proved. Marcus served historically as an example and reference partner; the new learner may freely choose it as the primary text. It has eight numbered chapters, not nine, and the project does not claim a separately certified whole-book proof completion for Marcus.

The 14 October list of nine hard packets is a dated baseline. Later source records mark eight resolved in their stated scopes, retaining cited inputs and source caveats. The snapshot keeps NM-HR-073 as an optional unfinished higher-local supplement. Its internal label does not assert a public unsolved problem. Neither publishing the English edition nor this release re-audits proofs or strengthens those historical claims.
